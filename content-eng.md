# The Fresnel Effect

The sun sets into the Baltic Sea every day without human intervention. Yet it is the specific observer’s angle of view that dictates this chromatic diversity: whether the water flares with the mirrored pastels of dusk or reveals the cold, steely depth of the sea. Physics here is inseparable from the optics of presence, born at the exact intersection of a human and the elements.

This is felt with particular clarity in Kaliningrad, where the metropolitan pendulum of commuting between suburb and city center gives way to a continuous transit between city and coast.

If Kaliningrad is a **school**, one of its primary lessons lies in the lived experience of this transit. Grasping this experience is impossible without both journeys: the road to the sea, framed by the anticipation of the encounter, and the journey back, essential for stepping out of pure contemplation.

Антон Ивченко / Rick Antilles
